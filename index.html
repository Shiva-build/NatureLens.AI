Four groups, one camera

Each group uses features that matter for that kind of living thing, so the explanation always matches what you photographed.

Plants and trees
Leaf shape, edge, vein pattern, flower structure, bark texture.
Birds
Beak shape, wing bars, tail length, eye ring, overall silhouette.
Insects and spiders
Wing count, antennae, leg joints, body segments, colour pattern.
Mushrooms and fungi
Cap surface, gill or pore layout, stem ring, colour changes.

Never eat a wild mushroom or plant based on an app. NatureLens is a learning aid. Check edible species with a local expert.

How it decides

  1. Look

    The photo is cleaned up and the subject is separated from the background.

  2. Compare

    Visible features are matched against thousands of reference species, and the closest candidates are ranked.

  3. Explain

    You get the top match, the features that supported it, and lookalikes to rule out.
